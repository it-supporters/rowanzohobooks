Structure
Multiple entities and branches

Locations: one organization

  • Branches and warehouses inside one organization
  • Shared chart of accounts, contacts and base currency
  • Transactions and reports filtered by location

Separate organizations

  • Own base currency, tax setup and financial year
  • Own bank accounts, users and e-invoicing connection
  • Intercompany handled with customer and vendor records
  • Group view through Analytics

Accounting records
Chart of accounts and general ledger
ConceptWhat it isWhy management cares
Chart of accountsAssets, liabilities, equity, income and expenses, with sub-accountsEvery report is built from it, so it is designed once, carefully
General ledgerEach transaction posted as debit and credit entriesOne source of truth, with drill-down from report to transaction
Manual journalsMonth-end adjustments such as accruals and depreciationDocumented, with attachments, and subject to approval
Recurring journalsEntries that repeat, such as a monthly prepayment releaseConsistent month-end postings without re-keying
Audit trailWho created or changed each entry, and whenAccountability for the CFO and external auditors
Financial reports
The four reports that matter first

Profit and Loss

Are we making a profit?

Income less expenses over a month, quarter or year.

Balance Sheet

What do we own and owe?

Assets, liabilities and equity on a given date.

Cash Flow Statement

Where does cash come from and go?

Cash in and out from operations, investing and financing.

Receivables and Payables Aging

Who owes us, and whom do we pay?

Open invoices and bills grouped by how overdue they are.
